## 1. Sacrificial anode or impressed current?

- A **sacrificial (galvanic) system** is its own power supply: the anode metal
  is the fuel. No rectifier, no mains power, little to go wrong — but the
  driving voltage is fixed by chemistry, so it suits coated structures and
  moderate current demand.
- An **impressed-current (ICCP) system** drives current from a rectifier
  through long-life anodes (HSCI, MMO/titanium). It scales to bare or large
  structures and high-resistivity soil, but adds powered equipment that must
  be operated and maintained.
- The choice follows from structure size, coating condition, soil or water
  resistivity, available power, and the maintenance the owner can plan for.

Full guide: https://yiruialloy.com/sacrificial-vs-impressed-current-cathodic-protection-guide/

## 2. Magnesium sacrificial anodes — the two potentials

- **High-potential magnesium (ASTM B843 grade M1C)** — for higher-resistivity
  soils where extra driving voltage is needed.
- **Standard-potential (AZ63-type)** — for lower-resistivity environments.
- Specify: alloy grade and standard, bare weight and dimensions (or standard
  model), steel core type and protrusion, bare or pre-packaged (backfill in a
  permeable bag), cable type/length, and the test documentation required.
- The steel core and packaging decide the tooling; composition is a furnace
  setting. Most enquiries over-specify chemistry and under-specify the core.

## 3. Magnesium vs zinc vs aluminum anodes

- Choose by environment first: soil / fresh water → magnesium;
  seawater and brackish → zinc or aluminum, per the governing standard
  (ASTM B843 for magnesium, ASTM B418 for zinc).
- Then check driving voltage against structure potential, current capacity
  per kilogram, and design life from net anode mass.

Comparison guide: https://yiruialloy.com/magnesium-vs-zinc-aluminum-sacrificial-anode-guide/

## 4. CP test stations — the wiring schedule is the specification

- A test station is a wiring schedule made physical: terminal board, body
  (GRP or steel), and sealed cable entries.
- State: station type (above-ground post / test box / junction box), terminal
  count with links and **shunts**, cable entries and sizes, body material,
  color/marking/lock (keyed alike across a batch on request), overall length
  and buried depth, base-plate or anti-rotation detail.
- A shunt reads current as a millivolt drop without breaking the circuit —
  it must be on the schedule when the station is built, not improvised later.

Specification guide: https://yiruialloy.com/cp-test-station-specification-guide/
GRP-or-steel body guide: https://yiruialloy.com/cp-test-post-grp-or-steel-guide/

## 5. Cu/CuSO4 reference electrodes

- The standard soil-side half-cell for structure-to-soil potential.
- **Portable** for surveys and commissioning; **permanent (long-life)** buried
  beside the structure and wired to a test station for monitoring without
  excavation.
- Read the spec sheet line by line: potential stability/drift, polarization
  behavior, working current density, temperature range.

Spec-sheet guide: https://yiruialloy.com/reference-electrode-spec-sheet-guide/
Product page: https://yiruialloy.com/products/reference-electrodes/

## 6. Impressed-current anodes (HSCI & MMO)

- You are buying geometry, mass, a cable, and a joint. Geometry sets surface
  area (output); **net weight** is the life budget and a goods-in check.
- For MMO: specify the rating, not the recipe — substrate grade, geometry,
  working environment, and the rated current + design life the maker must
  stand behind.
- The positive cable is the reliability clause: state conductor section,
  insulation build, tail length, and demand a factory-made, sealed, tested
  anode-to-cable joint. ICCP systems die of cable flaws, not spent anodes.

Specification guide: https://yiruialloy.com/impressed-current-anode-specification-guide/

## 7. Chinese GB casting grades vs Western equivalents

Chinese mills certify to GB/T 1173 (aluminum, "ZL") and GB/T 1177
(magnesium, "ZM"). Western drawings call ASTM/AA, EN 1706/1753, or JIS.
The mapping is **by chemical composition, not by name** — see
[GRADE-CROSSREF.md](GRADE-CROSSREF.md) for the full tables
(e.g. ZL111 ≈ 354.0 by composition, ZM1 → ZK51A and ZM2 → ZE41A are exact;
ZL205A has no Western equivalent).

## 8. What a quotable RFQ contains

Across every product family the pattern is the same — the enquiries that get
priced in one pass state:

1. the governing standard and grade (or the drawing);
2. geometry and net weight per piece;
3. the interfaces: steel core, cable, joint, terminals;
4. environment and installation condition;
5. quantity, packing, destination port, inspection/documentation requirement.

RFQ guides: https://yiruialloy.com/rfq-guides/
