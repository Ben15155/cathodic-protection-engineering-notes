# Magnesium bracelet-type sacrificial anodes — RFQ & specification notes

Practical specification notes for buyers and engineers, maintained by
**Jiaozuo Yirui Alloy Material Co., Ltd. (YIRUI Alloy)**, a magnesium and
aluminum light-alloy foundry in Jiaozuo, Henan, China. YIRUI makes magnesium
bracelet-type anodes in-house.

This note covers what a bracelet anode is in general terms and what a buyer
needs to state so an enquiry can be quoted in one pass. It deliberately
contains no sizes, weights or performance figures: those belong to the
buyer's design and drawing, not to a generic note.

---

## 1. What a bracelet anode is

- A **bracelet anode** is a sacrificial anode shaped to sit around a tubular
  structure, typically a pipe, rather than being buried beside it.
- It is normally made as **half-shells or segments**. Two or more segments
  are fitted around the pipe and joined to form a ring.
- Each segment is cast around a **steel insert or core**. The insert gives the
  segment its strength and provides the points where segments are connected
  to each other and to the structure.
- Segments are held in place either **mechanically** (bolted or clamped
  across the joints) or by **welding** the insert tabs, depending on the
  project specification.
- Electrical continuity to the structure comes through the attachment itself
  or through a separate bonding cable, again as the design requires.
- The coating on the pipe and how thick it is matter, because the anode
  has to sit on or against it. That is why the pipe and coating
  details come from the buyer's drawing.

Bracelet anodes are one form of magnesium anode. For the wider product family
see our [magnesium sacrificial anodes](https://yiruialloy.com/products/magnesium-sacrificial-anodes/) page.

## 2. Magnesium or another anode metal?

Whether magnesium is the right metal at all depends on the environment and
the governing design standard. That choice comes before the bracelet
geometry. We do not repeat the selection logic here. See the
[magnesium sacrificial anode selection guide](https://yiruialloy.com/magnesium-sacrificial-anode-selection-guide/)
for how to choose.

## 3. RFQ checklist — what a quotable enquiry states

1. **Governing specification or standard**: named by the buyer. The
   project specification controls; we quote against it.
2. **Alloy / grade**: as set out in the buyer's specification. Composition
   follows the spec rather than a catalog default.
3. **Pipe outside diameter and coating context**: pipe OD, coating type and
   coating thickness, taken from the buyer's drawing.
4. **Segment geometry**: number of segments per bracelet, segment shape,
   length and thickness, all from the drawing.
5. **Steel core / insert type**: the insert form, its material and how it
   protrudes for connection. The insert decides the tooling. Our
   [steel-core guide](https://yiruialloy.com/sacrificial-anode-steel-core-guide/)
   explains what to specify.
6. **Attachment method**: bolted/clamped or welded, as the buyer
   specifies. If bolted, state whether fasteners are to be supplied.
7. **Cable or bonding**: if the design calls for a bonding cable, give its
   type, length and connection method. If not, say so.
8. **Quantity**: number of bracelets (and spare segments, if any).
9. **Packing**: any packing or marking requirement.
10. **Delivery terms**: FOB Qingdao by default. CIF is also possible; state the
    destination port.
11. **Documents**: list what is needed. Material certificates are available
    on request; please state them in the enquiry rather than assume they
    come with every order.
12. **Drawing**: a 3D drawing, or a dimensioned drawing in **mm**, is needed
    for a quote.

## 4. Common gaps in bracelet enquiries

- **Pipe OD given, coating not.** Without coating type and thickness the
  segment inner profile cannot be confirmed.
- **"Standard bracelet" with no drawing.** There is no single standard
  geometry we can assume; the drawing defines the part.
- **Attachment method left open.** Bolted and welded designs use different
  inserts, so the choice has to be made before tooling.
- **Chemistry over-specified, insert under-specified.** As with other cast
  anodes, composition is set at the furnace; the insert and joint
  details are what drive the work.

## 5. Sending an enquiry

Enquiries are handled by email. Send the drawing and the checklist items
above. The structure of a complete enquiry for each product family is
set out in our [RFQ guides](https://yiruialloy.com/rfq-guides/).
